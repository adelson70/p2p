WeTransfer popularized dead-simple links—and a business model built on cloud retention and upsells. Privacy-first alternatives question whether the upload step is necessary at all.

## The upload tax

Every gigabyte crosses into someone else's bucket, scanned, logged, and billed. Even free tiers monetize attention and data.

## Browser P2P path

[PrivateDrop](/transfer) pairs two browsers, then streams chunks directly. No waiting for "processing" on a remote server.

## When cloud links still make sense

Asynchronous delivery to many strangers you will never pair with may still need e-mail plus cloud—accept the privacy trade openly.

## Feature checklist

Ask: retention period, password scope, jurisdiction, and whether content is scanned for ads or training.
