Accounts are convenient for vendors: they anchor billing, e-mail marketing, and cross-device history. They also create a durable identity that follows you across sessions.

## What we skip

Private Tools does not ask you to sign up to send a file or open a chat. Sessions are ephemeral pairings between browsers, not rows in a CRM.

## Analytics you will not find here

We are not building engagement funnels on your transfers. Without third-party ad trackers in the core flow, you avoid another layer of behavioral profiling.

## Trade-offs you should know

No account means no cloud restore of your pairing history across phones unless you export data yourself. That is intentional: convenience of central profiles trades against privacy.

## Practical tips

Use [PrivateChat](/communication/privatechat) only with people you trust, protect signaling text, and clear browser data on shared computers.
